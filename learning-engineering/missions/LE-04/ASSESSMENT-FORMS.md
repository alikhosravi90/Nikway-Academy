# Original parallel-task candidates / نمونه‌های اولیه آزمون
Status: original draft; expert/language/equivalence review pending. Not a validated instrument.
All task numbers are synthetic. Keep answers separate from participants until post-test completion.

## Shared prompt / دستور مشترک
In at most 500 words, define the problem and aim; propose a bounded, reversible trial with
an owner and stop rule; calculate before/after rates and percentage-point change; explain
what the comparison cannot show and an alternative explanation; justify the next action
and specify evidence for the next cycle. Label Plan, Do, Check, Act explicitly.

در حداکثر ۵۰۰ کلمه: مسئله و هدف را تعریف کنید؛ آزمایش محدود و قابل بازگشت با مسئول و
شرط توقف پیشنهاد دهید؛ نرخ قبل/بعد و تغییر واحد درصد را حساب کنید؛ محدودیت مقایسه
و یک توضیح جایگزین را بنویسید؛ اقدام بعدی و شواهد چرخه بعد را مشخص کنید.
چهار مرحله برنامه‌ریزی، اجرا، بررسی و اقدام را جدا نشان دهید.

## Form A / فرم الف
A fictional archive has 18 misfiled items among 150 checked items before a trial and
9 among 150 afterward. Proposed aim: <=7%. The staffing mix changed between periods.
بایگانی فرضی پیش از آزمایش ۱۸ پرونده نادرست از ۱۵۰ و پس از آن ۹ از ۱۵۰ دارد.
هدف پیشنهادی حداکثر ۷٪ است؛ ترکیب کارکنان بین دو دوره تغییر کرده است.

## Form B / فرم ب
A fictional service desk has 24 incomplete requests among 200 before a trial and
12 among 200 afterward. Proposed aim: <=7%. The mix of request complexity changed.
میز خدمت فرضی پیش از آزمایش ۲۴ درخواست ناقص از ۲۰۰ و پس از آن ۱۲ از ۲۰۰ دارد.
هدف پیشنهادی حداکثر ۷٪ است؛ پیچیدگی درخواست‌ها بین دو دوره متفاوت است.

## Rater-only key / راهنمای ارزیاب
Both forms: 12% before, 6% after, reduction of 6 percentage points (50% relative reduction).
The synthetic point estimate meets the 7% aim; it does not prove causal change or durable effect.
Do not require a single correct managerial decision: defensible adopt/adapt/stop/further-test
choices can earn full credit if justified and appropriately bounded.

## Operational scoring anchors / ملاک‌های نمره‌دهی
| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan / برنامه | No clear problem/aim | Problem or numeric aim incomplete | Clear problem and measurable aim connected to proposed change |
| Do / اجرا | No trial | Trial lacks owner, bounds or stop/rollback | Bounded trial with owner and explicit stop/rollback |
| Check / بررسی | Rates wrong or no evidence review | Correct rates but incomplete limitation/alternative | Correct rates and percentage-point change; limitation and alternative explanation |
| Act / اقدام | Unsupported next action | Reasoned action but no next-cycle evidence | Evidence-based next action and next-cycle measurement; no causal overclaim |

Scores are original proposed anchors, not established reliability or validity evidence.
For Persian scripts, review meaning rather than penalizing orthographic variants.
For accessibility accommodations, document the agreed conditions before testing and interpret
comparability cautiously; no automated accessibility compliance is claimed.

## Practical transfer / کاربرد عملی
With separate workplace permission, choose a low-risk real problem, document one bounded cycle,
attach an authorized artifact and explain limitations. No sensitive operational data in this repo.
Raters apply the same four criteria plus a separate authenticity/provenance check. If real action
is not authorized, record transfer as missing/not run; a simulation must not replace it silently.
