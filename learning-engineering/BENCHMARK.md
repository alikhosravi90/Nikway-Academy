### Comparison method (proposed, not scored)
For each individual reference record capture: referenceId, suppliedName, exactTitle,
issuer/author, edition, publicationDate, canonicalURL, accessedAt, claimLocator,
intendedUse, applicabilityLimits, referenceStatus, licenseStatus, licenseEvidenceURL,
allowedUses, reviewer, reviewedAt, and decisionRationale.
Unknown values remain UNKNOWN; do not invent URLs, dates or license identifiers.

Compare scope, evidence strength, bilingual suitability, competency/outcome alignment,
portability, accessibility coverage, implementation effort and reuse permissions.
Use UNKNOWN / unsuitable / partial fit / candidate fit for each dimension with a cited rationale.
No ranking or adoption decision is warranted until required evidence is reviewed.
Source reviewer and rights reviewer roles are proposed; named owners remain unassigned.
A verified reference can still have pending licensing. Summaries, translations, adaptations
and redistribution each require their own allowed-use assessment.
